The

“If it isn't engineered for scale and built to be observed, it isn't finished.”

I'm a backend engineer at Razorpay, working where fintech meets serious scale. Over the last few years I've pulled critical flows out of a PHP monolith into Go microservices, migrated tens of millions of records with zero downtime, and most recently owned and shipped Razorpay's self-hosted LLM observability platform to production.

My favourite problems span services, data and infrastructure at once — migrations, reconciliations, and platform work where correctness and reliability are non-negotiable. Before Razorpay I built core fintech systems at Paytm Payments Bank.
